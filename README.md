## 👋 About Me

> Full stack engineer working across three areas that usually live in separate job titles: **web** (MERN, Next.js, multi-tenant SaaS with Stripe and real-time WebSockets), **mobile** (React Native apps shipped end-to-end to the App Store and Google Play), and **AI systems** (LangGraph agents, hybrid RAG retrieval, semantic caching). I don't treat these as separate hats — most of my products need all three: a web dashboard, a companion mobile app, and increasingly, an AI layer that actually does something useful instead of just chatting.
